How it works

  1. Open AIV X and tap the voice button.
  2. Allow your microphone and start talking.
  3. Tap the circle to interrupt, or End to finish.

Questions

Is voice mode free?

Yes. It uses your device's built-in voice, so it doesn't use extra credits.

Which browsers work?

Most modern browsers, including Chrome, Edge, and Safari.

Is my voice recorded?

Your speech is turned into text to answer you. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Can I change the voice?

Yes. Turn on Natural voice in voice mode, or choose a voice in Settings.
